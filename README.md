# Freedom Unbound Mods

Mods for **Monster Hunter Freedom Unite** on the updated **Freedom Unbound launcher**.
Made by **DuduX42**. These releases support the USA version of the game (`ULUS10391`).

Only the latest version of each mod is available here. Use the download links below
or visit the [latest release page](https://github.com/Dudux42/FreedomUnboundmods/releases/tag/mods-2026-10-31).
The game and launcher are not included.

## Install

1. Download the mod ZIP. Do not extract it.
2. Open Freedom Unbound and drop the ZIP onto the **Mods** page.
3. Install the mod and turn it on.

Use Freedom Unbound **0.1.5** or newer. Weapon Upgrade Tree and Solo Guildhall Scaling need **0.1.6** or newer. Enable only one texture pack at a time.
Several mods have settings: press **F9** in game, pick the mod and open **Options**.
Each mod has a guide with more details if you need them.

## What each mod does

<a id="damage-numbers"></a>

### Just Damage Numbers

Shows how much damage each hit deals, right above the impact, with a small `*`
on critical hits. In **F9 Options** you can change the number size, the hit
colours and the strength of the white outline.

<a id="gathering-nodes"></a>

### Gathering Unbound

An icon appears above your hunter when there's something nearby to use: a
pickaxe for mining, a bug net for bugs, a knife for carving and a question mark
for regular gathering. It disappears when you draw your weapon or use up the spot.
Hold Circle (or your mapped equivalent) to keep gathering, and release to stop.
You still need pickaxes or bug nets where required.

In **F9 Options** you can turn each icon type on or off and change the icon size.

**Planned updates**

- Better icons for the gathering spots.

<a id="monster-hp"></a>

### Monster Health Bars

Shows the names and health bars of large monsters in your current area, stacked
at the bottom-left of the screen. Each bar also shows poison, paralysis, sleep
and stun buildup, and a notification appears when you break a part.

In **F9 Options** you can change the bar size, show HP as numbers, a percentage
or both, and pick the bar colour.

**Planned updates**

- Better animations on the health bars.

<a id="modern-camera"></a>

### Camera Unbound

Move the camera freely with the right stick during quests. The village and
Gathering Hall keep their usual camera. In **F9 Options** you can change the
horizontal and vertical sensitivity, invert either axis, set the smoothing and
turn monster targeting on or off.

With targeting on, press ZL/LT/L2 to face the selected monster and ZR/RT/R2 to
switch targets. With Monster Health Bars enabled, a target icon appears in its
health bar; otherwise, the icon appears beside the monster's name.

**Planned updates**

- Smoother camera controls.
- Fix camera angles that get snagged on level geometry.

<a id="key-quests"></a>

### Key Quest Indicator

Adds a gold star beside key quest names in the Village and Gathering Hall, making
it easier to see which quests you need to complete to progress.

<a id="fu-retextured"></a>

### Freedom Unbound HD

Gives the game a visual upgrade with sharper environments, menus, fonts, HUD,
hunter and equipment textures. This is a work in progress, with more of the game
being improved over time. Enable it as your texture pack in the Mods menu.
Press **F10** during gameplay to switch between HD and original textures.

Version **0.14.1** adds 12 new 8x textures for effects, a map, icons, UI and skin
tones, on top of 0.14.0's original-artwork item and equipment icons.
[Coverage and installation details](guides/fu-retextured.md).

**Planned updates**

- Keep improving the game's textures: armour sets, weapons, maps, monsters,
  NPCs, Felynes, icons and the HUD.

<a id="return-timer"></a>

### Return Timer

Shows how much time you have left before returning from a completed quest.
The countdown sits at the top-centre of the screen and turns red for the last
ten seconds.

**Planned updates**

- We are looking into a "Return now" button at the end of a quest to skip the timer entirely.

<a id="equipment-wishlist"></a>

### Equipment Wishlist

Keep track of the materials you need for equipment. At the smithy, select a weapon,
upgrade or armour piece and press ZR/RT/R2 to pin it. Press ZL/LT/L2 to remove its pin.

Press **F7** in the village, on a quest or in the Gathering Hall to open your
wishlist. It shows how many of each material you have, need and are still missing.
You get small notifications when you collect a needed material or have everything
required, and crafting the equipment removes its pin automatically.

**Planned updates**

- A better user interface overall.
- More responsive notifications.

<a id="controller-prompts"></a>

### Adaptive Button Prompts

Formerly Controller Prompts. Changes supported on-screen button prompts to match your keyboard bindings or
connected controller. It also shows the correct buttons for equipment pins and
the target camera. Prompts switch as you use your keyboard or controller.

Version **0.4.0** adds proper button art for Switch, PlayStation and Xbox
controllers and raised keycaps for the keyboard. Some written instructions and
tutorial images still show the original PSP buttons.

**Planned updates**

- Better button icons overall.

<a id="weapon-upgrade-tree"></a>

### Weapon Upgrade Tree

Highlight a weapon at the smithy and press **F8** to open its upgrade chart.
Follow the lines to see every upgrade path, including branches you haven't
unlocked yet. Select a card to see its Power, element or status, sharpness and,
for bows and bowguns, their shots, coatings and ammo in a side panel.

A green check marks weapons already in your item box. A gold marker means it's
the next upgrade from a weapon you own and you have the materials for it.
Browsing the chart never crafts anything or spends materials. Press **F8**,
Esc or Back to return to the smithy. Needs Freedom Unbound **0.1.6** or newer.

<a id="solo-guildhall-scaling"></a>

### Solo Guildhall Scaling

Large monsters have **35% less HP** in ordinary Offline Gathering Hall quests,
so the Hall is fairer when you hunt alone. Online Gathering Hall quests, Village
quests and small monsters keep their normal HP, and damage, rewards and break
thresholds are unchanged. Turn it on or off between quests, not during one.
Needs Freedom Unbound **0.1.6** or newer.

## Controls and combinations

| Mod action | Switch | Xbox | PlayStation | Keyboard default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Remove smithy pin / face selected target | ZL | LT | L2 | F6 |
| Pin smithy recipe / cycle target | ZR | RT | R2 | F7 |
| Open the Equipment Wishlist | | | | F7 |
| Open the Weapon Upgrade Tree (at the smithy) | | | | F8 |
| Switch HD and original textures | | | | F10 |
| Mod options | | | | F9 |

Use pin controls at the smithy and target controls during quests. You can change
the keyboard shortcuts in **Controls**. L/R shoulder buttons keep their usual functions.

- Target camera is included in **Camera Unbound**.
- Continuous gathering is included in **Gathering Unbound**.
- **Camera Unbound** integrates with **Monster Health Bars**, but also displays targets alone.
- **Adaptive Button Prompts** improves the displayed bindings for both Camera Unbound and
  Equipment Wishlist; their actions also work without it.
