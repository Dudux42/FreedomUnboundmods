# Freedom Unbound Mods

Mods for **Monster Hunter Freedom Unite** on the updated **Freedom Unbound launcher**.
Made by **DuduX42**. These releases support the USA version of the game (`ULUS10391`).

Only the latest version of each mod is available here. Use the download links below
or visit the [release page](https://github.com/Dudux42/FreedomUnboundmods/releases/tag/mods-2026-10-16).
The game and launcher are not included.

## Install

1. Download the mod ZIP. Do not extract it.
2. Open Freedom Unbound and drop the ZIP onto the **Mods** page.
3. Install the mod and turn it on.

Use an up-to-date launcher. Enable only one texture pack at a time.
Each mod has a guide with more details if you need them.

## What each mod does

<a id="damage-numbers"></a>

### Damage Numbers

Shows how much damage each hit deals, right above the impact. Numbers are bold
with a thin white outline: orange for normal hits, orange with a small `*` for
critical hits, and red for negative-affinity hits.

<a id="gathering-nodes"></a>

### Gathering Nodes

A green question mark appears above your hunter when you can gather, mine or
catch bugs nearby. It disappears when you draw your weapon or use up the spot.
Hold Circle (or your mapped equivalent) to keep gathering, and release to stop.
You still need pickaxes or bug nets where required.

**Planned updates**

- Show which items can be found at each gathering spot.
- Add monster carving to the list, including the items you can carve.

<a id="monster-hp"></a>

### Monster HP

Shows the names and health bars of large monsters in your current area, stacked
at the bottom-left of the screen. The bars appear as soon as you enter their area
and disappear when you leave it or reach the reward screen.

<a id="modern-camera"></a>

### Modern Right-Stick Camera

Move the camera freely with the right stick during quests. The village and
Gathering Hall keep their usual camera, and the D-pad still works normally.

The nearest large monster is selected automatically. Press ZL/LT/L2 to face it
instantly, or ZR/RT/R2 to switch targets. With Monster HP enabled, a target icon
appears in its health bar; otherwise, the icon appears beside the monster's name.

**Planned updates**

- Improve the overall camera controls.
- Improve the camera for ranged combat and special camera situations.

<a id="shared-guild-progression"></a>

### Shared Gathering Hall Progression

Complete Gathering Hall urgent quests together and let everyone earn completion
credit, even when they did not post the quest. Everyone who wants shared credit
must enable the mod. You still need to meet the usual rank and quest requirements.

This changes saved quest progress. Full multiplayer progression still needs testing.

<a id="key-quests"></a>

### Flag Key Quests

Adds a gold star beside key quest names in the Village and Gathering Hall, making
it easier to see which quests you need to complete to progress.

<a id="fu-retextured"></a>

### FU Retextured

Gives the game a visual upgrade with sharper environments, menus, fonts, hunter
and equipment textures. This is a work in progress, with more of the game being
improved over time. Enable it as your texture pack in the Mods menu.

**Planned updates**

- Improve the entire game: all male and female armor sets, all weapons, every map,
  NPCs, Felynes, icons and the HUD.

<a id="return-timer"></a>

### Quest Return Timer

Shows how much time you have left before returning from a completed quest.
The countdown sits at the top-center of the screen and turns red for the last
ten seconds.

<a id="buff-icons"></a>

### Buff Icons

Shows small icons beside your sharpness gauge for active timed item and Hunting
Horn buffs. Icons pulse when 30 seconds remain, then pulse and flash red at ten
seconds. When a buff ends, its icon disappears and the others move over.

The current icons are placeholders.

**Planned updates**

- Add a dedicated icon for every buff.
- Add permanent buffs, food buffs and negative effects.

<a id="equipment-wishlist"></a>

### Equipment Material Pin

Keep track of the materials you need for equipment. At the smithy, select a weapon,
upgrade or armor piece and press ZR/RT/R2 to pin it. Press ZL/LT/L2 to remove its pin.

You get small notifications when you collect a needed material or have everything
required. Ready-to-craft reminders also appear when you return to the village or
load your save. Crafting the equipment removes its pin automatically.

**Planned updates**

- Add a dedicated wishlist window showing pinned equipment and its required materials.

<a id="gathering-map-markers"></a>

### Gathering Map Markers

Shows gathering, mining and bug-catching spots on the map in your current area.
Used-up spots disappear. You can use it on its own or together with Gathering Nodes.

**Planned updates**

- Create better, dedicated icons for the map markers.

<a id="controller-prompts"></a>

### Controller Prompts

Changes supported on-screen button prompts to match your keyboard bindings or
connected controller. It also shows the correct buttons for equipment pins and
the target camera. Prompts switch as you use your keyboard or controller.

The current button icons are placeholders. Some written instructions and tutorial
images still show the original PSP buttons.

**Planned updates**

- Replace the placeholder buttons with actual controller icons.

## Controls and combinations

| Mod action | Switch | Xbox | PlayStation | Keyboard default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Remove smithy pin / face selected target | ZL | LT | L2 | F6 |
| Pin smithy recipe / cycle target | ZR | RT | R2 | F7 |

Use pin controls at the smithy and target controls during quests. You can change
the keyboard shortcuts in **Controls**. L/R shoulder buttons keep their usual functions.

- Target Camera is included in **Modern Right-Stick Camera**.
- Continuous gathering is included in **Gathering Nodes**.
- **Modern Camera** integrates with **Monster HP**, but also displays targets alone.
- **Controller Prompts** improves the displayed bindings for both Modern Camera and
  Equipment Material Pin; their actions also work without it.
- **Gathering Map Markers** and **Gathering Nodes** can be used together or separately.
